SyncDrive Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SyncDrive handles information when you use the SyncDrive iOS app (the “App”). The data controller for the App is Gökhan Çınar (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

SyncDrive does not require an account. Gameplay records and preferences are primarily stored on your device. The App uses Apple to process purchases, RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage entitlements, and Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (“UMP”) to provide ads and privacy choices.

1. Information handled by the App

Information stored locally

The App stores gameplay and preference information on your device, including:

  • scores, run history, selected pace, and progress statistics;
  • selected language, music and sound settings, onboarding state, and other preferences; and
  • cached purchase or entitlement state needed to decide whether ads and premium features should be available.

We do not receive this locally stored gameplay information unless you choose to include it when contacting support. You can remove local App data by using any available in-app reset controls or by deleting the App. Data restored from an iCloud or device backup is controlled by your Apple backup settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

The App uses RevenueCat to validate App Store receipts, restore purchases, determine subscription status, prevent purchase fraud, and grant the correct entitlements. RevenueCat may process:

  • App Store receipt and transaction information, product identifiers, purchase dates, subscription state, expiration or renewal state, and refund information;
  • an anonymous RevenueCat App User ID and app, device, operating-system, locale, currency, and technical information;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from IP address; and
  • the time the App or purchase service was last used.

SyncDrive does not require you to provide a name or email address to RevenueCat. If a future version adds an account or customer attributes, this policy will be updated before those features are used.

We use purchase information for App functionality, entitlement delivery, purchase restoration, subscription analytics, customer support, security, and fraud prevention. Monthly and annual SyncDrive Plus subscriptions unlock Plus features while active. The lifetime no-ads purchase is a non-consumable purchase and removes ads without granting other Plus content. Apple controls billing, introductory-offer eligibility, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and the purchase history associated with your Apple Account.

Advertising and consent information

The free version of SyncDrive may display interstitial ads between completed game runs. The App uses Google AdMob to request and display ads and Google UMP to present consent or privacy-choice forms where required.

SyncDrive is configured not to request personalized ads and does not present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission prompt. Depending on your location, consent choices, and the ad-serving mode available, Google may provide:

  • non-personalized ads, which are selected without using your past behavior for targeting but may use cookies, mobile identifiers, or other local storage for frequency capping and aggregated reporting when permitted; or
  • limited ads, which disable personalization and features that depend on local identifiers when the required consent for local storage is unavailable.

If Google’s consent system indicates that ads cannot be requested, the App does not send an ad request. The availability of an ad is never required to continue to the game result.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device’s general location;
  • device or app-scoped identifiers, subject to platform permissions and your consent choices;
  • advertising data, including ads requested, shown, dismissed, or interacted with;
  • App and product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • device, operating-system, app-version, language, and network information; and
  • crash logs, performance information, and other diagnostics used to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent invalid traffic, detect fraud, and keep the service secure.

Although we do not request personalized ads, some jurisdictions may treat certain advertising disclosures as “sharing,” “targeted advertising,” or a similar regulated activity. Where applicable, UMP provides the required choices. You can reopen the App’s privacy options from the privacy control in Settings whenever Google indicates that the control is required.

Diagnostics and support

The App does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service at the effective date of this policy. Google Mobile Ads may collect crash and performance diagnostics as described above. RevenueCat processes purchase-service information needed to diagnose purchase and entitlement issues.

If you contact us, we process the contact details, message, screenshots, diagnostic details, and other information you choose to provide so we can respond and resolve the request.

2. How and why we use information

We use information to:

  • provide gameplay, save preferences, and maintain local run history;
  • load non-personalized or limited ads and apply your consent choices;
  • validate purchases, grant entitlements, restore purchases, and manage subscription status;
  • maintain security, prevent fraud and invalid advertising traffic, and troubleshoot failures;
  • comply with law, App Store requirements, and requests from regulators or courts; and
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests.

Where applicable, our legal bases include performing a contract with you, complying with legal obligations, your consent, and our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the App. You may withdraw consent through the App’s privacy options where available. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

3. When information is shared

We disclose information only as needed to operate the App:

  • Apple Inc. processes App Store purchases, subscriptions, refunds, and Apple Account billing.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. validates purchase records and manages subscription and entitlement state.
  • Google LLC and participating ad providers deliver ads, manage consent, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and invalid traffic.
  • Service advisers and authorities may receive information when reasonably necessary for legal compliance, security, dispute resolution, or protection of rights.
  • Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of the App, subject to this policy and applicable law.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not provide personal information to data brokers. Advertising-related processing may still be classified as a sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under some privacy laws; applicable choices are provided through UMP.

4. Retention

  • Local gameplay and preference data remains on your device until you reset it, delete the App, or erase the device. Backup copies follow your Apple backup settings.
  • Consent records remain on the device until they expire, are reset, or are replaced by a later choice. Google may retain related records under its own policies.
  • RevenueCat and Apple retain purchase and subscription records as necessary to provide entitlements, process refunds, prevent fraud, meet accounting or legal obligations, and maintain transaction history. Their retention periods are controlled by their respective policies.
  • We normally retain direct support correspondence for up to 24 months after the request is closed, unless a longer period is necessary for security, legal compliance, or an ongoing dispute.
  • Aggregated or de-identified information that no longer identifies you may be retained for longer.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing; receive a portable copy of certain information; withdraw consent; or appeal a privacy decision.

You can:

  • change available advertising choices through Settings > Privacy Options when that control is required;
  • manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings;
  • restore eligible purchases from the App’s purchase screen;
  • delete locally stored App data by deleting the App or using available reset controls; and
  • contact us to request access to or deletion of information we control, including a request to delete the corresponding anonymous RevenueCat customer record where legally and technically possible.

We cannot delete purchase history controlled by Apple. We may need information sufficient to locate and verify an anonymous purchase record before acting on a request, and we may retain records when required by law or needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

You may also complain to the privacy or data-protection authority in your country.

6. International transfers

Apple, RevenueCat, Google, and their service providers may process information in the United States and other countries that may have different data-protection laws from your country. They use contractual, organizational, and legal safeguards for international transfers where required. See their policies for details.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. Purchases are processed through Apple, and the App does not store full payment credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

SyncDrive is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where required. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s device, advertising, and purchase settings.
