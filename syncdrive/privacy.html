Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

SyncDrive ("we", "our", "the app") is a dual-lane reaction game developed by Gokhan. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

SyncDrive does not collect personal data or transmit gameplay data off your device:

  • No accounts or personal information. The app does not require registration, login, or personal details.
  • Local gameplay data. Your best score and run history are stored only on your device.
  • Local preferences. Speed, music, sound effects, language, and onboarding choices are stored only on your device using standard iOS storage.

Purchases and Advertising

SyncDrive does not contain subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertising.

Analytics and Tracking

SyncDrive does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track your activity, build user profiles, or share data with advertisers.

Data Shared with Third Parties

SyncDrive does not send data to us or to third parties. Apple may separately provide developers with diagnostic or usage information when a user has chosen to share analytics with Apple and app developers. That information is handled by Apple under its own privacy policies and settings.

Children's Privacy

SyncDrive does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require personal data to function, and no gameplay data is transmitted from the device.

Data Retention and Deletion

All SyncDrive data is stored locally on your device. To delete it, delete SyncDrive from your device. We do not maintain server-side app data.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no personal app data for us to access, correct, or delete. Your local gameplay data remains under your control.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hi@gokhan.pw.
